## Everything has a rhythm

In film, what matters is not just what we see but when we see it. Interfaces work in a similar way: the order of information can help us understand a situation or completely break our focus.

## A well-timed cut

A good transition leaves enough context. We know where we came from and what happens next. We do not have to piece the whole story together again.

## Room to breathe

Silence can be as expressive as dialogue. Empty space on a page can play a similar role: it helps us notice what actually matters.

## One story at a time

When everything competes for attention, it is hard to follow any thread. It helps to choose one central idea and build the experience around it, step by step.
