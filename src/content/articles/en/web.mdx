## Room for your own voice

A personal website can be small. It does not have to compete for every second of attention. It is enough to have something to say and help readers find what they came for.

## Beyond the default template

A distinctive typeface, some breathing room, and an unexpected accent can say more than an elaborate animation. Form should support the content and give it character.

## Quiet reading

Text needs good contrast, a comfortable line length, and rhythm. These small decisions make it easier to stay a little longer and understand what the author means.

## Conversation over reach

Maybe the most valuable result of publishing will be one good conversation. That is why getting in touch should be simple, and leaving should be as easy as arriving.
